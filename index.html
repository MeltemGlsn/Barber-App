FAQ

Mobile barber FAQs

Which areas does your mobile barber service cover?

We cover Portsmouth, Southsea, Fratton, Cosham, Drayton, Fareham, Gosport, Havant, Waterlooville and Hayling Island. Message us if your area is not listed.

How much does a mobile barber cost in Portsmouth?

Prices start from £20 for a beard trim and £35 for a haircut, with no extra travel fee inside our main areas. Every cut includes a consultation, hot towel finish and full clean-up.

What do I need to prepare?

Just a chair and a little space near a plug socket. We bring everything else, including a floor sheet, and leave the room spotless.

Do you cut hair for elderly clients and care homes?

Yes. We regularly visit elderly and less mobile clients at home and in care homes around Portsmouth. We are DBS checked and fully insured.

Do you do weddings and groom parties?

Yes. We come to your venue, hotel or home on the morning of the wedding and get the groom and groomsmen ready. Message us for a group quote.

Is there a travel fee?

Travel is free within our main areas. A small fee may apply for addresses further out, and we always confirm it before booking.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash after your cut.
